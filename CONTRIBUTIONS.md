# Contribution and AI assistance

Scaramouche and Wanderer are personal projects directed by Abriana Meazzo ([@Kittybri](https://github.com/Kittybri)). Development has used AI coding agents for implementation, debugging, tests, deployment assistance, and documentation.

The project owner's contribution includes choosing the product behavior, supplying concrete failure reports, setting constraints such as minimal-change repairs, and deciding which work to pursue. AI assistance contributed code and technical analysis. This portfolio does not imply that the owner independently authored every line or independently performed every diagnostic command.

The task fixtures, graders, reference implementations, and portfolio documents in this repository were created with AI assistance. They were checked by executing starter/reference acceptance validation. That establishes consistency with the stated task contracts, not independent peer review or evidence of a model's coding ability.

## Evidence boundaries

- The PDF case study reports an observed incident and records verification limits.
- The turn-ownership case study describes a design and runnable specification.
- Synthetic task examples are not verbatim production source snapshots.
- Thresholds and rubrics are explicit engineering choices, not experimentally calibrated research findings.
- No model leaderboard or measured performance study has been conducted for this portfolio.

## Reviewing the work

A useful technical discussion can follow a failure report to the task contract, then the tests and reference implementation. Reviewers can inspect alternatives, explain why a solution fails, or change a requirement and extend its tests. Ownership should be supported by that understanding rather than by a claim of unaided authorship.
